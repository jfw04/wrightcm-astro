Address plus intended use in; a written go / no-go / conditional out in two business days. Slab and drainage, clear height, power and gas, water and sewer, refrigeration feasibility, docks, zoning and AHJ posture, with the two or three things that would kill it and roughly what each costs to fix. One page. Forwardable. Unlimited.

Register a named client and building; we confirm in writing within one business day and that registration holds for 24 months. We will not represent your client in any real estate capacity, will not refer them to another broker, and will not pursue their other locations without looping you in. If they call us directly about a new building during your window, we tell you the same day and treat it as your deal.

The questions brokers actually ask

Straight answers.

Is this going to slow my deal down?

No. The program runs on your clock, not ours, 48 hours to a written screen, five days to a site walk. If we can't hit a date we tell you the same day and pay the penalty.

Are you going to scare my client out of the building?

We deliver a decision, not a list of fears: go, no-go or conditional, with a number and a path. A "no" from us in week one beats a "no" from a lender in week twelve, and you get credit for catching it.

My client isn't ready for a contractor yet.

Then don't introduce us as one. Introduce us as the feasibility resource you use on industrial buildings. Most of what we do happens before anyone would normally call a GC, which is the whole point.

I already have GCs I call for pricing.

Keep them. This isn't a bid. Nothing obligates your client to hire WCM, and the screens, walks and ROM budgets stay free even if they hire someone else. Use us for the food, cold storage and USDA questions general contractors guess at.

What if my client never builds?

You still collect the $500 Qualified Look Fee on any registered deal that reaches a completed feasibility review, and you still got a free answer that moved your transaction.

Will you go around me?

No, and it's in writing, both directions, for 24 months. Cutting out the person who brings us deals would be the dumbest thing we could do with this program.

Can I even accept this? My brokerage has rules.

Usually yes, this is a construction referral fee, not a real estate commission. But that call belongs to your broker of record. Tell us at registration whether to pay you, your firm, both, or a charity you name, and we'll paper it that way. We'll send your compliance team the terms directly if that helps.

Does this cost me anything or lock me into anything?

No fee, no exclusivity, no term, no minimum. Register one deal or twenty. Stop any time.
